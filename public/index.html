FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Solar Fox Services and our route-based model.

We schedule multiple homes in the same neighborhood on the same cleaning day. This reduces our travel time and fuel costs significantly. We pass those savings directly to you — so you get the same professional service at up to 20% less than standard rates, just by being on an active route.

Most customers save between 15–20% compared to a standard single-home booking. The exact discount depends on how many homes are on your route and how close they are to each other. Your quote will always show the route discount applied clearly.

We recommend cleaning every 6 months in most areas — spring and fall. In dusty regions like Phoenix, Tucson, or Las Vegas, quarterly cleaning (every 3 months) keeps output at its peak. Dirty panels can lose 25–30% of their energy production.

Yes. Solar Fox Services is fully licensed, bonded, and carries comprehensive general liability insurance. All technicians are background-checked and trained on safe roof and panel cleaning procedures. We carry proof of insurance and will provide it upon request.

We use purified, deionized water with biodegradable, pH-neutral cleaning solution that is safe for all panel manufacturers and warranties. We never use harsh chemicals, abrasive tools, or pressure washers that could damage your panels or void your warranty.

A standard home with 20–24 panels typically takes 45–60 minutes. Our Premium Detail service takes slightly longer due to frame and edge work. You do not need to be home — just make sure the water access point is unlocked if needed.

No, you do not need to be home. We just ask that you let us know if there are any access issues (locked gates, dogs, etc.) when booking. We will send you a text when we arrive and again when we are done, along with a photo of your clean panels.

We stand behind our work with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you are not completely happy with the results, contact us within 48 hours and we will return to re-clean at no charge. We consider a job done only when you are satisfied.
